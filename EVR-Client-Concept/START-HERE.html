What is functional in this prototype?

  • Eight responsive views, plus a collapsible mobile navigation.
  • Selectable hero cards and reversible gallery, perspective and floating-note chapters.
  • Date validation and transfer of the chosen date/service into the enquiry form, without checking availability or reserving anything.
  • Playable local illustrated motion samples in project and testimonial layouts; one preview at a time, with close, native playback controls and pause on leaving the viewport.
  • Five-stage phone/process illustration driven by scroll or step buttons, with a static selectable fallback.
  • Three-page team-role carousel, expandable testimonial-placement notes and homepage FAQ.
  • Portfolio category filters and project-detail previews on labelled placeholder cards.
  • Customer-facing estimate with sample coverage/output calculations and transfer into a demo brief.
  • Required-field validation and a demo enquiry confirmation. No request is sent or stored.
  • Two staff quotation presets with editable quantities/rates, discount and tax.
  • Validation of invalid numeric inputs and over-large discounts.
  • One local browser draft, with save/load feedback. Storage may be browser-dependent for local files.
  • Quotation preview and browser Print / Save as PDF.

Not implemented or claimed: authentication, server storage, real enquiries, EVR client footage, genuine testimonials, cloud sync, a client portal/mobile app, automatic WhatsApp/email delivery, payments, live availability, booking or a live CMS. The public estimate is simplified, not a complete event/crew rules engine. Demo amounts are not EVR's approved prices.

Motion design: expanded from the supplied recording

The V4 homepage uses a pinned, multi-stage scroll story. The opening copy exits, the media collects into a stack, six cards spread around a central EVR statement, two original perspective panels appear, creative-brief notes float into place, then wedding/corporate choices arrive. Scrolling backwards reverses each phase. Native scrolling remains intact; Skip intro exits to the main services. Mobile has a shorter timeline and its own gallery arrangement. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript modes use a static hero without the scroll runway.

The separate process section pins the phone and text. Native scroll continuously changes the phone's three-dimensional tilt, screen crossfades, progress and notification position while highlighting the relevant step. Step buttons additionally seek the same sequence. Reduced motion and short phone screens keep the buttons usable without a pinned runway. Watch the scroll-only process demonstration, view the hero storyboard or watch the complete homepage walkthrough. PDFs show static layouts, not animations.

Interactive hero cards

Click or tap either visible side card to bring it to the front. The previous front card moves into that side position; the third card stays in place. The swap also works with keyboard Enter/Space and in reduced-motion mode. Card selection is disabled once the scroll-story gallery takes over, and your selection is restored when you scroll back. No automatic cycling or hero playback is enabled.

The hero still uses image placeholders, as requested. Its containers support image/video sizing; hero video sources, poster frames, playback, sound and controls will be configured when actual footage is supplied.
