6. Quote requests: information, not a public rate card

Confirm the choices customers should see, which are required, and which should be removed because EVR does not offer them.

  • Wedding: event categories; maximum shoot days; date/session options; photographers, cinematographers, drone, live streaming and display services; pre-wedding durations; films, teasers, invitations, albums, raw-file requests and storage media.
  • Corporate: shoot categories; crew and kit; production days; brand films, reels, YouTube/podcast edits, captions, thumbnails, output formats and intended usage.
  • Customer details: confirm name, phone, email if needed, project/couple name, city, date, brief and preferred callback time. Decide what is required versus optional.
  • Enquiry handling: who receives it, notification email, callback phone, business hours and an achievable response expectation. No response-time promise until approved.
  • Delivery method: direct WhatsApp and email hand-offs are implemented. Visitors review the prefilled message and send it from their chosen app; automatic email delivery and a staff inbox are not configured.
  • Consent: approve the callback/privacy wording and whether enquiries may be retained. Agree retention and deletion rules.

The enquiry form prepares a complete WhatsApp or email message for the visitor to review and send. EVR must confirm availability, scope and any booking directly; the form does not make reservations or send messages without the visitor's action.

7. Policies, permissions and launch access

  • Approved privacy notice, booking terms, payment/cancellation/rescheduling policy and copyright/usage terms. Obtain appropriate legal review; generic demo text is not a finished policy.
  • Agreed delivery and revision policies, raw-file availability, archive/retention arrangements and how travel or specialist requirements are discussed.
  • Permissions for wedding guests/couples, employees, speakers, client logos, venues, licensed music and featured minors as applicable. Do not send private identity documents as content proof.
  • Domain name and owner, registrar/DNS access by invitation, hosting preference/account and who pays ongoing costs. Keep domain ownership with the client.
  • Business mailbox setup and scoped access to any email provider or lead-management service selected for the live enquiry flow.
  • Optional Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics access via account invitations. Decide tracking and consent requirements first.
  • Target launch date, one final approver and sign-off on content, mobile layouts, enquiry destinations and public pricing policy.

Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment-card information in a shared content folder or chat. Use account invitations and appropriately limited access. Secrets belong in protected deployment configuration, not the website files.

8. How to organise the handover

  1. 01_Brand: logo masters, colours, fonts/licences and business details.
  2. 02_Weddings: one subfolder per project, each with Photos, Videos and project information.
  3. 03_Corporate: one subfolder per project, with matching Photos, Videos and project information.
  4. 04_Showreels_Reels: wedding/corporate showreels, two vertical reels and suggested hero timecodes.
  5. 05_Team_Reviews: portraits, biographies, genuine reviews and permitted client logos.
  6. 06_Copy_Policies: services, FAQs, process notes, request-form choices and approved policies.
  7. 07_Approvals: a simple asset list: filename, project, permission status, restrictions, preferred cover and final approver.

Use Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or another mutually approved delivery method, with access granted to the developer. Name files clearly, for example Wedding_Project01_Hero_Landscape_01.jpg and Brand_Project01_Film_Master.mp4. Keep originals; website compression and responsive crops are handled separately. No private assets are uploaded automatically.

9. What can launch first?

Smaller first launch: 36 photographs (18 wedding, 12 corporate, 6 team/studio) and 6 videos (1 wedding showreel, 2 wedding-side films, 1 corporate showreel, 1 brand film and 1 vertical reel). This needs selective reuse and a smaller portfolio. Hide any unsupported work, team or testimonial placements rather than inventing content.

Recommended complete launch: the 60-photo / 12-video kit above, 6 project stories, 4-6 genuine reviews where available, approved business/copy/policy information and a tested live enquiry workflow. No need for a unique asset on every repeated page placement.

Suggested collection order: first logo/contact details and a short approved media shortlist; next the six project folders and copy; then permissions, policies and launch access. This allows the visual direction to be finalised while the remaining content is collected.
