THE WAY WE WORK

We never ask you
to pose.

WHAT IT MEANS

We don't stop a ritual for a photo. We don't ask you to hold a smile while the purohit waits. We don't line the family up for an hour under harsh lights. Your wedding runs the way it was always going to.

HOW IT WORKS

Portraits still happen: fast, natural, in the light that's there. The rest of the day we're part of the family: talking, laughing, standing where the moment is about to happen. That's why the photos look like the day felt.

BEFORE WE MAKE IT

A clearer brief.
A better start.

What should we include in our brief?

Your brand, audience, main message, where the content will be used, preferred shoot date and the deliverables you have in mind. Reference links help communicate your direction.

Can one shoot support different formats?

It can be planned that way. Discuss landscape films, vertical reels and short extracts before shooting. Crew, framing, edit quantities and final versions need to be included in the agreed scope.

Does this include social media management or paid ads?

Production services include brand films, marketing reels, YouTube and podcasts. Posting, paid media, SEO, websites and automation are not included unless EVR separately agrees to offer them.

How are pricing and delivery decided?

EVR will discuss scope, pricing and delivery with you on a call. No rates or automatic totals are displayed online.

How are client credits and results shared?

Project details, client feedback and results are shared only after they have been confirmed and approved for publication.
