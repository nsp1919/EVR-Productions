Major scenes follow the visitor's scroll position. Scrolling backwards restores the earlier stage.

Lighter movement elsewhereSection reveals, gentle media movement and scroll-progress heading accents.

09 / How the concept becomes the website

Approve the direction.
Then build with real stories.

The visual concept is ready for review. Production content, live connections and launch checks follow approval.

STEP 01

Layout sign-off

Confirm both visual directions, page order, navigation, motion approach and the quote-request journey.

STEP 02

Content integration

Replace illustrations with approved EVR media. Finalise copy, project stories and genuine reviews.

STEP 03

Live implementation

Optimise media, connect the agreed enquiry channel and prepare the approved hosting and domain.

STEP 04

Review & launch

Test mobile layouts, media, motion and enquiry delivery. Obtain final approval before publishing.
